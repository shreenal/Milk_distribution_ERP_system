## Purpose

The Cash Settlement module records the physical accounting of money at the end of a business day: cash returned from routes broken down by denomination, expenses incurred on routes, cash handed directly to the office by employees, and cash deposited into banks.

## Responsibilities

- Return a single settlement payload for a paper (all four sections).
- Upsert `cash_route_settlement` and its `cash_route_expense` children per order sheet.
- Upsert `cash_direct_collection` per `(paper, employee)`.
- Upsert `cash_bank_deposit` per `(paper, bank)`.

## Business rules

- Write windows differ per channel (from `WorkflowStateService`):
  - `route-expenses`: `NIGHT_SUBMITTED` or `REOPENED` (`canEditRouteExpenses`).
  - `route-denominations`: `NIGHT_SUBMITTED` only (`canEditRouteDenominations`).
  - `direct-collections`: `NIGHT_SUBMITTED` only (`canEditDirectCollections`).
  - `bank-deposits`: `NIGHT_SUBMITTED` only (`canEditBankDeposits`).
- Uniqueness: one `cash_route_settlement` per `order_sheet_id`, one `cash_direct_collection` per `(paper, employee)`. `cash_bank_deposit` has no `(paper, bank)` uniqueness — multiple deposits to the same bank on the same day are permitted.
- Cash settlement is a **precondition of morning submit**, not an after-the-fact step. On `POST /papers/:paperId/submit-morning`, `PaperValidationService` invokes `CashSettlementValidationService.validateMorningSubmitReadiness`, which enforces two reconciliations per paper: (1) for every sheet, `route net cash = route denominations total`; (2) bank deposits must not exceed the office cash available, and the deposited denomination counts must not exceed the available (route \+ direct-collection) denomination stock. Any mismatch blocks the transition.
- After reopen, cash settlement is treated as **historical**. Route denominations, direct collections, and bank deposits stay frozen (their `canEdit…` helpers whitelist `NIGHT_SUBMITTED` only); only route expenses stay editable. The morning-submit reconciliation is not re-run on re-finalize. Any gap between the persisted cash and the recalculated expected cash surfaces on the settlement summary as `Reconciliation Difference` instead of being rewritten.

## Dependencies

- `PrismaService` — reads and writes the four cash tables.
- `WorkflowStateService` — status-gated writes.
- Master data — `master_expense_type`, `master_bank`, `master_employee`.

## Endpoints

| Method | Path | Role | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `GET` | `/cash-settlement/:paperId` | EMPLOYEE | Return all four sections for the paper |
| `POST` | `/cash-settlement/:paperId/route-expenses` | EMPLOYEE | Save route expenses |
| `POST` | `/cash-settlement/:paperId/route-denominations` | EMPLOYEE | Save route denominations |
| `POST` | `/cash-settlement/:paperId/direct-collections` | EMPLOYEE | Save direct collections |
| `POST` | `/cash-settlement/:paperId/bank-deposits` | EMPLOYEE | Save bank deposits |

## Request flow

```text
POST /cash-settlement/:paperId/*
  → CashSettlementController
  → CashSettlementService.<method>
       upsert into the corresponding table keyed by
         cash_route_settlement.order_sheet_id, or
         cash_direct_collection (paper, employee), or
         cash_bank_deposit (row per entry with bank_id)
```

## Database tables

- `cash_route_settlement`, `cash_route_expense`, `cash_direct_collection`, `cash_bank_deposit` — see [Cash Settlement data model](/data-model/cash-settlement).

## Lifecycle

```text
NIGHT_SUBMITTED   → POST /cash-settlement/:id/{route-expenses, route-denominations, direct-collections, bank-deposits}
MORNING_SUBMITTED → read-only for cash-settlement (route-expenses too)
FINALIZED         → read-only via GET
REOPENED          → POST /cash-settlement/:id/route-expenses (only channel unlocked again)
```
