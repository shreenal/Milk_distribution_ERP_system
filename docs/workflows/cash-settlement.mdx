**Cash Settlement** is the closing money step of the day. Its four write channels run alongside the morning shift — the paper must be in `NIGHT_SUBMITTED` for three of them and remains partially editable after that. Its job is to physically account for every rupee collected: what came back on the routes, what the office collected directly, what got spent on the routes, and what ended up in the bank.

## Preconditions

- Paper is in `NIGHT_SUBMITTED`. This is when all four channels are open. After the paper moves to `MORNING_SUBMITTED` or `FINALIZED`, every channel is locked.
- If the paper is in `REOPENED`, only `route-expenses` accepts writes. Route denominations, direct collections, and bank deposits stay locked (their `canEdit…` helpers whitelist `NIGHT_SUBMITTED` only).
- Client collections and route expenses should already have been noted down on paper by the drivers before you begin entering them into the ERP.

## The four inputs

Cash Settlement has four write endpoints under `POST /cash-settlement/:paperId`:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Route denominations">
    `POST /cash-settlement/:paperId/route-denominations`

    Per order sheet (per route), the physical currency breakdown returned by the route: counts of ₹2000, ₹500, ₹200, ₹100, ₹50, ₹20, ₹10 notes and total coin value. Stored in `cash_route_settlement` keyed by `order_sheet_id` (unique).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Route expenses">
    `POST /cash-settlement/:paperId/route-expenses`

    Per order sheet, one or more expense rows: `(expense_type, amount, remarks)`. Stored in `cash_route_expense` linked to the route's `cash_route_settlement`. Expense types are master data (`master_expense_type`).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Direct collections">
    `POST /cash-settlement/:paperId/direct-collections`

    Per employee, the physical currency breakdown of cash they handed directly to the office (outside of the route). Stored in `cash_direct_collection` keyed by `(order_paper_id, employee_id)`.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Bank deposits">
    `POST /cash-settlement/:paperId/bank-deposits`

    Per bank, the physical currency breakdown of cash deposited plus a deposit reference number. Stored in `cash_bank_deposit`.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

The read endpoint `GET /cash-settlement/:paperId` returns all four in one payload.

## How the numbers reconcile

The system does not enforce a reconciliation; it displays the pieces and expects the Admin to verify. The manual reconciliation is:

```text
Sum of client_collection.cash_collection across all sheets
  − Sum of cash_route_expense.amount across all routes
  = Sum of cash_route_settlement denominations (in ₹)
    + Sum of cash_direct_collection denominations
    + Sum of cash_bank_deposit denominations (if deposits happen out of route cash)
```

If a mismatch shows up, the fix is typically:

1. Recount physical cash on the disputed route.
2. Correct denominations via a re-save.
3. Or, if the mismatch is because a client's cash entry was wrong, use Admin Save on the collection first, then re-save the denomination.

## Diagram

```text
Route ends → driver hands cash to office
Employee → POST /cash-settlement/:paperId/route-denominations
Employee → POST /cash-settlement/:paperId/route-expenses
Employee → POST /cash-settlement/:paperId/direct-collections
Employee → POST /cash-settlement/:paperId/bank-deposits
Admin → GET  /cash-settlement/:paperId   (verify totals)
Admin → POST /papers/:paperId/finalize   (only after all inputs are entered)
```
