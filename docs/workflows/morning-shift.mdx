The **morning shift** is the second half of the day in the ERP. It begins when the paper is in `NIGHT_SUBMITTED` and ends when an employee submits the morning entry, moving the paper to `MORNING_SUBMITTED`.

Cash settlement is part of the morning shift, not a step after it: route denominations, direct collections, and bank deposits are only writable in `NIGHT_SUBMITTED`, and the morning-submit readiness check refuses to close the shift until route-cash and bank-deposit reconciliation succeed. See [Cash Settlement](/workflows/cash-settlement) for the details of each channel.

## Preconditions

- The paper is in `NIGHT_SUBMITTED`. If it is in `REOPENED`, the same morning endpoints are available (see [Reopen Workflow](/workflows/reopen-workflow)).
- The night vehicle allocation exists — otherwise the morning purchase step has nothing to reconcile against.

## Step 1 — Employees plan the morning vehicle allocation

Some products are dispatched on morning routes, not night routes. Employees add or update morning-session allocations by calling the same endpoint used at night, and the service scopes writes to the morning `vehicle_allocation_paper`:

- `POST /vehicle-allocations/:paperId/vehicle-allocations`

The service uses `(paperId, MORNING)` to isolate morning allocations from night ones.

## Step 2 — Employees record purchases from the dairy

At the dairy, vehicles are loaded. Employees enter what was actually picked up per `(vehicle, product_link, delivery_session)`:

- `POST /purchases/:paperId`

Each entry contains `vehicleId`, `deliverySession`, `distributorId`, `category`, `productId`, and `purchasedQty` (`SavePurchaseDto.entries[]`). `product_link_id` is resolved by the service from `(distributorId, productId)`; `purchase_rate` is resolved from the active `distributor_product_rate`; `purchase_amount = purchasedQty × purchase_rate`; `gatepass_date` is derived from the brand's `gatepass_date_policy` (`SAME_DAY` = paper sale date; `PREVIOUS_DAY` = sale date minus one). The service:

- Creates the `purchase_paper` if one does not already exist for the paper.
- Upserts `purchase_entry` keyed by `(purchase_paper, delivery_session, vehicle, product_link)`.
- Compares each entry against its planned `vehicle_allocation.allocated_qty` for the same `(vehicle, distributor, category, product, deliverySession)`. If they differ, a matching entry in `acknowledgements` (with a `PurchaseVarianceReason`) is required; otherwise the save is rejected.

## Step 3 — Vehicles deliver, employees record delivered quantities

On the route, drivers actually deliver the milk. Back in the system, employees enter the delivered quantity per client per product:

- `POST /orders/sheet/:sheetId/morning-save`

For each item:

- The paper's status must be `NIGHT_SUBMITTED` or `REOPENED`. Otherwise the request is rejected.
- A night order item for the same `(client, product_link)` must exist. You cannot deliver something that was not ordered.
- The service updates `delivered_qty`, `final_selling_rate`, `final_gst_percentage`, `final_gst_amount`, `final_taxable_amount`, and `final_bill_amount` on the `order_sheet_items` row.

## Step 4 — Employees record morning collections

Just like night collections, but for money received during the morning route:

- `POST /collections/sheet/:sheetId/milk/morning-save`
- `POST /collections/sheet/:sheetId/non-milk/morning-save`

The service upserts the same `client_collection` row keyed by `(sheet, client, category)` — it merges morning amounts into whatever was already saved at night.

## Step 5 — Employees record tray movements at clients

For each client, per tray type, employees record trays taken and trays returned during the morning delivery:

- `POST /trays/sheet/:sheetId/save`

The service upserts `client_tray_transaction` rows keyed by `(sheet, client, tray_type)` and computes `closing_balance = opening_balance + trays_taken − trays_returned`.

## Step 6 — Employees record dairy tray movements

At the dairy, the vehicles return with empty trays. Employees record the balance per `(vehicle, tray_type)`:

- `POST /dairy-trays/:paperId/save`

The service creates the `dairy_tray_paper` if it doesn't exist, then upserts `dairy_tray_transaction` rows.

## Step 7 — Employee records cash settlement

Before morning submit can close the shift, the day's cash must be entered while the paper is still in `NIGHT_SUBMITTED`. This includes route denominations, route expenses, direct office collections, and bank deposits. See [Cash Settlement](/workflows/cash-settlement) for the exact channels and their write windows.

## Step 8 — Employee submits the morning entry

Once morning orders, morning collections, purchases, trays, dairy trays, and cash settlement are complete, an employee calls:

- `POST /papers/:paperId/submit-morning`

The service runs a cross-module **readiness check** through `PaperValidationService` before the transition. It refuses to close the shift if any of the following is incomplete or inconsistent:

- Morning entries missing or with unrealistic quantities on any sheet.
- Client tray movements missing on any sheet.
- Morning collections missing on any sheet.
- Purchases incomplete.
- Dairy trays incomplete.
- Distributor-transfer rules missing for any required supplier→owner pair implied by the day's deliveries.
- Cash-settlement reconciliation failing: route denominations must equal route net cash on every sheet, and bank deposits must not exceed available office cash or the available denomination stock.

Only after readiness passes does the service:

- Validate the transition `NIGHT_SUBMITTED → MORNING_SUBMITTED`. Note that `submit-morning` is **only** valid from `NIGHT_SUBMITTED`; when the paper is in `REOPENED`, the same morning-save endpoints are open but the paper re-finalizes via `POST /papers/:paperId/finalize` (transition `REOPENED → FINALIZED`) rather than by re-running `submit-morning`. See [Paper States](/workflows/paper-states) for the exact transition table.
- Update the paper's status and stamp `morning_entry_submitted_at`.
- **Trigger auto-generation of distributor transfers.** See [Distributor Transfer](/workflows/distributor-transfer) for how this works.

## After morning submit

- Order, purchase, tray, morning-collection, and cash-settlement endpoints are now closed for regular employees. Only Admin-only collection fields (`online_collection`, `bank_deposit`, `admin_remarks`) accept writes.
- The paper is now visible to Admins for finalization review.

## Diagram

```text
Employee → POST /vehicle-allocations/:paperId/vehicle-allocations  (MORNING session)
Employee → POST /purchases/:paperId
Employee → POST /orders/sheet/:id/morning-save
Employee → POST /collections/sheet/:id/{milk,non-milk}/morning-save
Employee → POST /trays/sheet/:id/save
Employee → POST /dairy-trays/:paperId/save
Employee → POST /cash-settlement/:paperId/{route-expenses,route-denominations,direct-collections,bank-deposits}
Employee → POST /papers/:paperId/submit-morning  → readiness check → status becomes MORNING_SUBMITTED
                                                                       (auto-generates distributor_transfer[])
```
