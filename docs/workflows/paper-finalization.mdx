**Finalization** is the Admin action that closes a day. It transitions the paper from `MORNING_SUBMITTED` (or `REOPENED`) to `FINALIZED`. After that, no module accepts writes for this paper until it is reopened.

## When to finalize

Finalize the paper only after these are complete:

- Delivered quantities entered for every client that was scheduled to receive product.
- Morning collections entered on every route.
- Trays and dairy trays counted.
- Cash settlement complete: route denominations, route expenses, direct collections, and bank deposits entered.
- Distributor transfers reviewed. They were auto-generated at morning submit, but you can inspect them at `GET /distributor-transfer/:paperId` and regenerate if anything was corrected in `REOPENED` state.
- Admin-only collection fields reviewed and any corrections applied via `POST /collections/sheet/:sheetId/{milk,non-milk}/admin-save`.

## The review sequence

Most admins follow this sequence on the operations screen:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Delivery Summary">
    Call `GET /delivery-summary/:paperId` (or open the [Delivery Summary screen](/frontend/delivery-summary-screen)) to see billing-group and product-level totals. Spot-check totals against manual paper records or the dairy gatepass.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Cash Settlement">
    Call `GET /cash-settlement/:paperId` (or open the [Cash Settlement screen](/frontend/cash-settlement-screen)). Confirm route denominations sum to expected collections minus expenses; confirm direct collections and bank deposits match physical evidence.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Distributor Transfers">
    Call `GET /distributor-transfer/:paperId`. If the transfers do not reflect the day's actual flows (for example because a rate or supply rule was fixed after morning submit), call `POST /distributor-transfer/:paperId/generate` to regenerate the rows. `WorkflowStateService.canEditDistributorTransfers` returns true only for `REOPENED`, so in practice the frontend restricts manual regeneration to that state. The service method itself does not gate on paper status, but you should not rely on that.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply admin-only corrections">
    If any collection row needs an amount corrected or an admin remark added, use `POST /collections/sheet/:sheetId/{milk,non-milk}/admin-save`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finalize">
    Call `POST /papers/:paperId/finalize`. The service validates the transition, stamps `finalized_at`, and updates status.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What finalization does at the database level

Before transitioning, `PaperValidationService.validateFinalizeReadiness` runs the finalize readiness check: on every order sheet, the admin-only collection fields must be recorded. If any sheet still has admin collections pending, finalize is refused.

Once the readiness check passes, `WorkflowStateService.validateTransition()` accepts the transition and:

- Updates `order_paper.status` to `FINALIZED`.
- Stamps `finalized_at` with the current timestamp.
- Leaves `reopened_at` and `reopen_reason` untouched (they only get cleared on a fresh workflow, never automatically).

There is no bulk recomputation on finalize. All amounts on `order_sheet_items` and `client_collection` were already computed at their respective save endpoints.

When re-finalizing from `REOPENED`, the same admin-collections readiness check runs. Cash-settlement reconciliation is **not** re-run: after reopen, cash settlement is treated as historical and the recalculated gap is exposed as Reconciliation Difference rather than being blocked.

## What finalization blocks

Once the paper is `FINALIZED`:

- Every order, collection, purchase, tray, dairy tray, vehicle allocation, and cash settlement endpoint that writes returns a business-rule error naming the current status.
- Every read endpoint continues to work as normal.
- Reopening is the only way to unlock writes again.

## Common mistakes

<Warning>
  Finalizing without regenerating distributor transfers after edits leaves stale transfer records. If you edit collections or purchases in `REOPENED` and did not regenerate transfers before finalizing, the transfer rows may not match the final numbers. Regenerate first.
</Warning>
