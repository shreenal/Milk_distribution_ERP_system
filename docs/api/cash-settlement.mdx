title: "Cash Settlement API — End-of-Day Cash Reconciliation"
sidebarTitle: "Cash Settlement"
description: "Record and retrieve end-of-day cash reconciliation data including route denominations, route expenses, direct collections, and bank deposits."
---

The Cash Settlement API captures every rupee accounted for at the end of the day. Cash arrives from four distinct channels: route-side denominations counted per vehicle, route expenses paid out of that same cash, direct office collections (walk-in payments), and bank deposits. Each channel has its own table but they all share a single `order_paper` foreign key so the ledger balances on a per-day basis.

`ADMIN` role is required for every save endpoint. Read is available to both roles.

---

## GET /cash-settlement/paper/:paperId

### Purpose

Returns the complete cash reconciliation for a paper, spanning all four channels.

### Response

<ResponseField name="route_settlements" type="array">
  One `cash_route_settlement` per (vehicle, session) with denomination breakdown.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="route_expenses" type="array">
  Cash paid out on-route (fuel, tolls, adjustments).
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="direct_collections" type="array">
  Walk-in payments taken at the office.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="bank_deposits" type="array">
  Amounts deposited to bank accounts on behalf of the day.
</ResponseField>

## POST /cash-settlement/paper/:paperId/route-settlement

### Purpose

Records the denomination-level cash count from each vehicle at end of shift. Denominations are stored as counts (`d_2000`, `d_500`, `d_200`, `d_100`, `d_50`, `d_20`, `d_10`, `d_coins_amount`) rather than a single total, so the physical count is auditable.

### Access control

`ADMIN` role.

### Request body

<ParamField body="items" type="array" required>
  Entries with `vehicle_id`, `delivery_session`, denomination fields.
</ParamField>

---

## POST /cash-settlement/paper/:paperId/route-expense

### Purpose

Records cash paid out from the route bag before it was returned. Common categories: fuel, tolls, driver advances.

### Request body

<ParamField body="items" type="array" required>
  Entries with `vehicle_id`, `delivery_session`, `category`, `amount`, and optional `remarks`.
</ParamField>

---

## POST /cash-settlement/paper/:paperId/direct-collection

### Purpose

Records office-side cash receipts not attached to a specific route. Typically walk-in payments from clients settling account balances.

### Request body

<ParamField body="items" type="array" required>
  Entries with `client_id`, `amount`, and optional `remarks`.
</ParamField>

---

## POST /cash-settlement/paper/:paperId/bank-deposit

### Purpose

Records amounts deposited to a bank account for the day.

### Request body

<ParamField body="items" type="array" required>
  Entries with `bank_id`, `amount`, and optional `reference` (deposit slip number).
</ParamField>

### Validation and errors (all save endpoints)

| Scenario | HTTP status | Detail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Caller not `ADMIN` | `403 Forbidden` | Role check |
| Paper is `FINALIZED` | `400 Bad Request` | Reopen the paper first |
| Negative amount | `400 Bad Request` | Must be `>= 0` |
