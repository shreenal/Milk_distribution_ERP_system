---
title: "Milk Distribution ERP"
description: "Daily operations backbone for a milk distribution business. NestJS + Prisma + PostgreSQL backend, Electron/Vite frontend, anchored on one Order Paper per day."
---

The Milk Distribution ERP records every step of a distributor's day: night orders, morning deliveries, purchases from the dairy, vehicle allocation, client and dairy tray movements, cash settlement, and inter-distributor transfers. Everything hangs off a single daily document called the **Order Paper**, whose status (`DRAFT` → `NIGHT_SUBMITTED` → `MORNING_SUBMITTED` → `FINALIZED`, with an optional `REOPENED` loop) is the master switch for which module can write.
