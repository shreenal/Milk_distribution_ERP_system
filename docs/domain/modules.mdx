The ERP is organized into a small number of modules, each owning one clear business responsibility. Every module is bounded by the paper lifecycle: it can only write to the database when the paper is in the right status. This page lists each module, the role that operates it, the paper statuses in which it accepts writes, and where to go deeper.

## Module map

| Module | Primary role | Writes allowed when paper is in | What it produces |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Paper](/modules/paper) | Admin (create, finalize, reopen), Employee (submit) | Drives the transitions between all states | The `order_paper` record and its status transitions |
| [Orders](/modules/orders) | Employee | `DRAFT` (night save), `NIGHT_SUBMITTED` or `REOPENED` (morning save) | `order_sheet_items` — night and morning quantities per (client, product) |
| [Collections](/modules/collections) | Employee (night/morning), Admin (admin-save) | `DRAFT`, `NIGHT_SUBMITTED`, `MORNING_SUBMITTED`, `REOPENED` (fields differ) | `client_collection` — cash, cheque, online, office amounts per client per category |
| [Purchase](/modules/purchase) | Employee | `NIGHT_SUBMITTED` or `REOPENED` | `purchase_paper` and `purchase_entry` — what each vehicle collected from the dairy |
| [Vehicle Allocation](/modules/vehicle-allocation) | Employee | `DRAFT` (night session), `NIGHT_SUBMITTED` (morning session), `REOPENED` (both sessions) | `vehicle_allocation_paper` with assignments and per-product quantities per session |
| [Trays](/modules/trays) | Employee | `NIGHT_SUBMITTED` or `REOPENED` | `client_tray_transaction` — trays taken and returned at the client level |
| Dairy Tray Tracking | Employee | `NIGHT_SUBMITTED` or `REOPENED` | `dairy_tray_paper` and `dairy_tray_transaction` — trays returned to the dairy per vehicle |
| [Cash Settlement](/modules/cash-settlement) | Employee | `NIGHT_SUBMITTED` (all four channels); `REOPENED` (route expenses only) — route denominations, direct collections, and bank deposits are frozen after `NIGHT_SUBMITTED` | Route denominations, route expenses, direct collections, bank deposits |
| [Distributor Transfer](/modules/distributor-transfer) | System-triggered by Employee | Auto-generated inside `submit-morning`; `POST /distributor-transfer/:paperId/generate` gated by `canEditDistributorTransfers` = `REOPENED` only | `distributor_transfer` rows for cross-distributor supply reconciliation |
| [Delivery Summary](/modules/delivery-summary) | Employee (read-only) | Any status | Billing group and product-level totals for the paper |

## Reading order

If you are new to the codebase, read the module pages in this order because each depends on the ones before it:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Paper">
    [Paper](/modules/paper) owns the lifecycle that every other module gates on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Orders">
    [Orders](/modules/orders) is the primary data-entry module and drives billing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Vehicle Allocation, then Purchase">
    [Vehicle Allocation](/modules/vehicle-allocation) plans the load; [Purchase](/modules/purchase) records what was actually loaded at the dairy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collections">
    [Collections](/modules/collections) records money in from clients through the day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trays">
    [Trays](/modules/trays) records physical crate movement, which is a running balance separate from money.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cash Settlement and Distributor Transfer">
    [Cash Settlement](/modules/cash-settlement) closes the money loop; [Distributor Transfer](/modules/distributor-transfer) closes the multi-distributor supply loop.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delivery Summary">
    [Delivery Summary](/modules/delivery-summary) is a read-only rollup once the day is done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Master data

The modules above operate on the day's paper. They all read from master data tables managed outside the daily cycle:

- Clients and groups — see [Clients & Groups](/data-model/clients-groups).
- Distributors and their rules — see [Distributors](/data-model/distributors).
- Products, brands, product groups, product links, rates — see [Products](/data-model/products).
- Vehicles and drivers — see [Vehicles](/data-model/vehicles).
- Tray types and tray rules — see [Trays](/data-model/trays).

Master data changes should not happen mid-day. If they do (a new client, a new rate), they take effect for papers created after the change.
